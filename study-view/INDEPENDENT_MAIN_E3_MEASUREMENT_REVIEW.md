# Independent main39 and E3 measurement review

Verdict: **REVISE** the comparison's metadata and inferential framing; the recorded descriptive denominators, selected-output bindings, and warning-count arithmetic pass the checks below. This is a measurement-integrity judgment, not a publication-acceptance judgment. Reviewed independently on 2026-09-29 without reading author-conclusion documents or other reviewer reports. No model calls or new scans were launched.

The strongest criticism is that these records do not establish a stable improvement in detector quality. `FINAL39_SUMMARY.json` counts versions with any warning, not independently adjudicated target findings. Native versus no-internal gives 23 versus 21 vulnerable versions with warnings, but 18 versus 13 fixed versions with warnings: precision 0.561 versus 0.618, F1 0.575 versus 0.575342, PDS 5 versus 8, and fixed-warning reports 123 versus 68. Thus the extra version hits accompany more fixed-side warnings and no F1 improvement. `REPEATED_ABLATION_STATUS.json` gives five positive, three negative, and 28 tie/other paired decodes; no subject is positive in all three decodes. Seven subjects have mixed directions and five tie throughout. A generalized quality or stable causal-superiority claim would exceed this evidence.

Denominators and chronology are recoverable. Main `MATRIX_FREEZE.json` names 39 unique subjects, 78 condition cells, six reused pairs, and 66 new cells. All 39 rows in each of starting/native/no-internal/Knighter were inspected. All selected candidate and paired-result hashes matched their referenced files; all selected paired results were execution-valid without infrastructure errors or scan-failure artifacts. These subjects had prior experimental use; the freeze explicitly says all are experimental samples. There is no supported development/holdout split to invent.

E3 `e3-selected-repeats/MATRIX_FREEZE.json` records 12 subjects, two additional decodes and 48 new cells. Decode one is exactly reused from main, yielding 36 paired decodes/72 condition observations, not 36 independent bugs or 72 newly executed trials. I checked each repeated selected checker hash and each reported alert pair against its cell result; referenced non-starting paired-result hashes also matched. Subject-level grouping is necessary for uncertainty estimates. The repeat subset and shared model alias limit generalization, even though ties and negative directions are retained.

These are not 39 fresh 32-response executions.
