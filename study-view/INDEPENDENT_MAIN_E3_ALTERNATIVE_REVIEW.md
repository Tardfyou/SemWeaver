**Verdict: REVISE evidence interpretation.** Pass the bounded descriptive observation that the two recorded pipelines produce different retained version-warning portfolios. Stop claims that these results establish target recall, a uniform native advantage, or a causal benefit of a particular evidence component. This is an interpretation judgment, not an acceptance prediction.

I inspected the frozen matrix, selection record, raw summary rows, E3 comparisons, both retention runners, selected checker diffs, and selected attempt prompts/responses and validations. I did not read author findings, response narratives, or other reviews; run models; or change experiments. Paths below are relative to the project root. Alert pairs are vulnerable/fixed.

## Strongest criticism and chronology

The central alternative is **selection on warning presence/count plus changes in detector scope**, rather than recovery of the motivating vulnerability. The adoption rule requires only a nonzero vulnerable count and either recovery from zero or fewer fixed warnings (`native-state-20260928/run_cell_v43.py:17–18,78–84`; `final-native-20260928/run_profile_cell.py:169–176`). It does not bind a report to the target, preserve the vulnerable diagnostic set, or distinguish suppression from repaired semantics. A zero-warning start may therefore acquire arbitrarily many fixed warnings. Controlled-hit preservation applies only to native and is empty in the inspected G12/G19/G24 selected results; it provides no positive target guarantee there. Continuations also start from the latest attempt, not necessarily the retained best, so outcomes reflect adaptive search trajectories and stopping.

`final-native-20260928/SELECTION.json` records choosing wrapper_matching over state_key after inspecting six samples: qualified gains 2 versus 1. Those six remain among all 39, with their selected native outputs reused. `MATRIX_FREEZE.json` records native revision `16b57931…` and reused controls from `813e5567…`; this is historical reuse, not a uniformly fresh same-revision experiment. Different revisions alone do not prove behavioral confounding; control equivalence requires its recorded audit. There is no basis to invent an independent development/holdout split. E3 reuses main outputs as decode 1 and adds two decodes on the same 12 subjects (`e3-selected-repeats/MATRIX_FREEZE.json:first_decode`). It estimates repeat behavior on these subjects, not generalization to new bugs.

## What the results support and still require

`FINAL39_SUMMARY.json` reports native/control 23/21 vulnerable-hit subjects, 18/13 fixed-warning subjects, 5/8 positive-zero-fixed pairs, and 123/68 fixed reports. Thus the portfolio trades more version hits for more fixed noise; F1 is essentially equal (0.575 versus 0.57534). G12 contributes 73 of native's 123 fixed reports. The other apparent main negatives G05/G23/G38 retain their own 0/0 starts while control recovers warnings; G01 recovers 2/2 from 0/0 versus control 2/0. These are not evidence that native erased previously retained vulnerable hits.

E3 has 36 complete pairs on 12 subjects: 28 tie/other, 5 positive, 3 negative; five subjects always tie/other and seven vary. No subject has three consistently positive or negative comparisons. G08's negative decode retains its original 2/2 while control improves to 1/1. Treat subject, not decode, as the independent unit.

Outstanding requirements are interpretive: separate initial→retained→between-arm differences; expose selection/reuse; name version hits and warning reductions accurately; classify G12 as noisy and G24 as unresolved preservation/scope; and retain repeated directions. No new experiment is required for this bounded report.

**Methodological clarification:** Realized calls (150/133) are outcomes or mediators of the shared progress/stopping policy, not independently assigned budget confounds. Their difference alone does not invalidate the descriptive contrast or require equal-call adjustment. The contrast compares complete native-visible versus withheld pipelines, including native-only quality gating and downstream adaptation. Bundling prevents attributing it separately to static facts, dynamic observations, or the guard; target-preserving causality remains unestablished by warning counts. Selection and reuse limit confirmatory independence; stochastic draws and E3 variation limit certainty about expected directions. These limitations do not erase the recorded overall pipeline differences. The REVISE verdict concerns interpretation, not validity of that bounded descriptive comparison.
