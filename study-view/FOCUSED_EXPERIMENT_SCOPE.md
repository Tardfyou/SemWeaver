# User-approved auxiliary-experiment reduction

The latest user instruction authorizes a defensible reduction to finish the
remaining work. This supersedes the earlier request to retain the entire189-cell
model matrix. Existing outputs, including negative and interrupted results,
remain preserved; no favorable result determines the new subject list.

## Unchanged primary evidence

- All39 frozen CSA subjects remain in the main comparison: native, no-internal,
  and the actual/equivalence-audited KNighter refinement workflow.
- GPT-6-Luna high, the32-response ceiling, starting checkers, patches, scorer,
  adoption rules and completed records remain unchanged.
- E3 keeps two additional decodes on the original12 in both conditions:
  12×2×2=48 additional cells. Their first decode comes from the main study.
  These are repeated observations of the same subjects, not independent bugs.

## Focused model sensitivity

- E4 uses the original12 IDs in E3_E4_PREPARATION.json, fixed before current
  full-cohort outcomes. There is one decode for each of three models:36 cells.
- The12 are the previously retained experimental subset, not a newly drawn
  random population sample or an independent unseen-project benchmark.
- The exact IDs are G04, G08, G09, G13, G14, G15, G19, G24, G25, G36, G37,
  G38. This subset omits buffer-overflow and out-of-bound categories; it is
  not a category-complete benchmark. prepare_e3_e4.py preserves the earlier
  repeated12 identities from scanfix_v26_corrected_summary_v1/RESULT.json.
- All three conditions retain the2-response and65536-output ceilings. Existing
  Luna high results are reused only when the exact selected subject and protocol
  match; GLM profiles explicitly request high effort and use the Anthropic-native
  output_config shape as well as the flat provider shape. Old default/flat-only
  GLM records are separate, not silently pooled into the focused comparison.
- Complete uncapped observer evidence and diagnostic parity remain mandatory.
  Observer capacity changes do not edit scored checkers or add case/file/line
  filters. Infrastructure failures are repaired and retained, not zero-scored.

## Scheduling and reporting

Future launches in the former full E4 queues are stopped. Already in-flight
requests finish, and their raw replies and costs are retained as additional
observations outside the focused matrix. No active model call was cancelled.

The manuscript can describe sensitivity to three model configurations on this
fixed12-subject subset. It cannot infer all39 cross-model stability, unseen-project
generalization, universal native-evidence superiority, statistical significance,
or independent target recall from this reduced experiment. Report all36 planned
cells and execution coverage; do not select each model's best decode.

E4 tests native only, with one decode per model. It cannot establish that the
native-versus-no-internal advantage persists across models; model and decoding
variability are not separately estimated here. The two-response E4 setting is
distinct from the main/E3 32-response setting. Present E4 as a limited-budget
configuration-sensitivity check, not evidence of equal computation across models.

Final source/data/paper agreement, English artifact verification, meaning-
preserving polish and F258 visual alignment remain required before publication.
