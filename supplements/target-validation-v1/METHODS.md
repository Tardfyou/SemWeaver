# Scope, phases and limitations

## Original comparison and follow-up selection

All 39 generated-only CSA subjects remain in the original comparison. Native versus no-internal refinement has six favorable directions, five unfavorable directions and 28 ties/other outcomes. The complete eleven non-ties, not a favorable subset, receive source/diagnostic audits. The remaining 28 are not target-audited. Warning recovery and lower fixed-side report burden are operational outcomes, not independent bug counts or proof of target correctness. Fixed-side reports are inspection burden, not uniformly confirmed false-positive bugs.

The audit contains 362 condition-expanded retained HTML occurrences (254 distinct HTML files), seven additional log-only records and 88 condition/side cells. Duplicated report bytes are explicit aliases, not new findings. Source support, conditional premises, reported-path support and runtime proof are distinct.

Four mechanisms (G12, G19, G24 and G25) have six correlated vulnerable/fixed source-property pairs each: 48 sources, 96 unique checker scans. Fixture constructors could see source and patches; source-property labels were frozen before detector scans. These are controlled reductions, not 48 independent real-world vulnerabilities or unseen-site generalization. G12 models annotations rather than executing a race; G24 models logical retirement rather than demonstrating physical kernel UAF. Later semantic interpretation does not alter frozen endpoint-line scores.

G19 additionally has four frozen lowering controls: an initial eight-scan report-serialization failure is retained separately from eight healthy replays. Four benign controls distinguish cleanup reads from harmless callbacks or writes. They are not pooled into the primary 96.

## Secondary semantic interpretation

The condition-masked packet contains 68 validation records, 588 physical scan records and 608 represented fixture occurrences. Deduplication yields 272 review observations (224 healthy, 48 compile-unscored), 158 healthy zero-diagnostic observations and 16 distinct checker artifacts. These different units are not interchangeable sample sizes. Natural code/fixture clues can reveal conditions: masking is not perfect blinding.

AI-assisted source/checker/path review is not independent human ground truth or runtime validation. G19 and G25 use different conventions for observations containing mixed diagnostic judgments; their observation labels are not pooled into an accuracy score. The public post-review `PHASE_BINDINGS.json` supports phase/code/fixture joins without releasing the masking seed or private operational metadata. No primary label or endpoint score is retrospectively changed.

Recognition repairs can activate useful checks while leaving object, owning-frame, guard or path obligations unsatisfied. Examples include G19's premature callee-exit warning and G25's reports on explicitly non-NULL paths. Conversely, some source-supported guard reports fall outside the frozen endpoint list. Report existence, target-line presence and bounded semantic support are therefore reported separately. This is an evidence-accounting conclusion, not a newly implemented acceptance oracle or a universal disadvantage of internal evidence.

## Reproduction and disclosure boundary

R0 verifies delivered file membership, sizes and SHA-256 values. R1 recomputes recorded endpoint counts from raw PLISTs and reconstructs specified joins and tables. It preserves null/unscored values. Replaying recorded annotations does not validate their semantic truth. Binaries, container layers and historical service execution are not reverified; their retained hashes are historical metadata. No end-to-end R2/R3 reproduction is claimed by these commands.

Internal rating discussions, goal/handoff documents, editing strategies, private account configuration, credential files, compiled plugins and build caches are excluded. Mixed editorial/protocol documents are not exported wholesale; scientific conditions are restated here and remain anchored in raw run plans, freezes, requests, responses, candidate sources and results. Historical manifests with omitted members are explicitly marked public binding projections rather than falsely presented as untouched, self-contained closures. Public-derivative transformations and original identities are recorded in the export manifest. Identifying path changes do not rename `/f/` analyzer paths or alter diagnostic line/column ranges.

The original main artifact and its 143 payload parts are preserved separately. This supplement is self-contained for its supported recorded-output checks; it does not depend on access to the original workstation or unpublished API credentials.
