# Evidence-strengthening revision

This revision integrates the completed source audit of all eleven non-tied
comparisons, controlled source-property perturbations, repeated selected-clue
decisions and secondary source/path interpretation. It preserves all original
E1--E4 scores and all unfavorable outcomes.

The main distinction is between recognizing the backend representation and
establishing the target's object, scope, guard and path obligations. Internal
evidence can help the first without establishing the second. The paper reports
both useful repairs and counterexamples; it does not claim universal precision
improvement, unseen-site generalization or a newly implemented acceptance oracle.

Strict outputs, bounded post-response interpretation and separate compiler-only
completion are explicitly separated. The new terminal table includes all three
draws per arm for both checkpoints. Compile failures, schema exceptions and extra
withheld-arm continuation effort remain visible. Secondary AI-assisted judgments
do not replace primary scores or constitute independent human ground truth.

The original writing-use disclosure is retained verbatim and identified as the
original disclosure; current drafting and evidence-synthesis assistance is stated
separately. Two supporting repair-validation references were added after checking
their primary bibliographic identities and relevant claims.

All 61 citation keys resolve. The final manuscript has 18 body pages and four
reference pages. User-owned Figures 1 and 3 remain at 70% and Figure 2 at 100%.
Existing data tables and algorithm are preserved. The new table uses the existing
grouped-rule style and does not color mechanical recovery as semantic correctness.

The target-validation supplement provides raw evidence, explicit phase bindings,
anonymization provenance and portable R0/R1 checks. These reconstruct recorded
measurements without calling a model, compiler or analyzer. Full end-to-end reruns
and independent human validation are not claimed by those checks.
