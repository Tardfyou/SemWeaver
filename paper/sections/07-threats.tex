\noindent\textbf{Internal.}\quad
An error in evidence collection, LLM editing or validation could produce a spurious improvement.
We retain raw outputs, code hashes, failed attempts and unavailable observations; all scored checker edits are automatic.
Configuration selection used six experimental subjects, with compatible executions reused in E1.
Native context also bundles several components and includes a fixture guard absent from no-internal, although that guard rejects no E1/E3 candidate.
The checkpoint intervention varies selected clues while retaining other native context.
Its post-response adapter and compiler-only continuations are separate from the strict first decisions, and equal reply ceilings do not ensure equal realized compute.
Model aliases, omitted temperature fields and the absence of a fixed seed leave decoding variance uncontrolled.

\noindent\textbf{Construct.}\quad
The primary metrics describe warnings on vulnerable and fixed revisions under the same patch-related scope, not target-level precision and recall or confirmed false-positive bugs.
G08 concerns a different leak path, and G24's retained warnings concern another lifecycle.
Final-line matching cannot distinguish two objects or a feasible and infeasible path on the same line; the follow-up adds AI-assisted interpretation of source and paths without changing primary scores.
These judgments lack independent human calibration; a source-feasible completion is not a runtime witness.
G19 and G25 use different mixed-observation conventions, so their labels are not pooled as accuracy.
Some historical zero-scan records lack complete stdout.

\noindent\textbf{External.}\quad
The 39 subjects are selected representatives from one generator's Linux artifacts.
The noisy and silent starting groups exercise different refinement behavior, including KNighter's no-report policy.
The 11 non-ties, four perturbation mechanisms and two checkpoints form nested, selected scopes; G12 models access annotations and G24 logical retirement rather than executed races or physical kernel UAF.
The motivating fixtures informed its edits.
The model study uses three decodes per configuration on the same 12 subjects, with no bounds cases; CodeQL remains one patch-local example.
The controlled source replay covers three selected original subjects and does not represent independent natural evolution.

\noindent\textbf{Conclusion.}\quad
Repeated decodes and transformed fixtures are correlated observations, not additional independent bugs.
Native and no-internal $F_1$ are nearly tied, and no repeated subject benefits comparatively in all three decodes.
Our findings explain local repairs and failures; they do not estimate their prevalence or establish a rule predicting when native evidence will help.
