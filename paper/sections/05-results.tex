\subsection{RQ3: Internal-Evidence Sensitivity}

Corrected initial evidence contains 193 records on all 39 subjects: 81 native-backed, 51 analyzer outputs and 61 source-derived records. All 61 source-derived records are patch-diff-derived, not source-window fallbacks. Native types comprise 45 guards, 27 state-transition and 9 allocation-lifecycle records; contextual fields attached to a native-backed record are not necessarily native facts. Dynamic availability has a separate denominator: 103 of 150 unique native-chain attempts have usable hash-bound observations; 11 are unavailable due to candidate compilation, 18 unsupported context, 13 zero scoped callbacks, 1 reproduced candidate crash and 4 capped optional observations. None establishes safety from absence.

The full 39 comparison has six native comparative benefits, five disadvantages and 28 ties/other outcomes. Changes include cleanup-wrapper recognition in G19, nested dereference/binding recovery in G25, and null/region/frame handling in G24. G19 adds \texttt{\_\_free\_kfree} to a cleanup predicate, yielding $15/0$ versus control's $0/0$; 15 reports are not 15 bugs. Conversely, control recovers symbolic-region handling in G23 and persistent-field release recognition in G38 without native records. G12 drops CPU-role restrictions, explaining its broad matching and noise. Code differences are observable, but a record's presence or an LLM explanation alone does not prove edit causality.

\subsection{Qualitative Findings}

The automatic motivating checker retains AST traversal and adds actual-capacity reasoning, with 14 received replies and fixture-guided corrections. Its 114 finite successes are separate from the main one-response checker's 74/114. The case ledger retains all 11 non-tied main comparisons, selected diffs and intermediate outcomes. Three positive selected candidates have usable dynamics (G19/G24/G25), and three use static records with unavailable dynamics (G09/G12/G35). All six gains therefore cannot be credited to dynamic traces. The findings support conditional checker completion and auditable tradeoffs, not universal precision improvement, proof of target preservation or unseen-family recall.
