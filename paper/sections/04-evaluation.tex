We evaluate automated patch-related warning recovery and report reduction, not whole-kernel bug discovery. Checker outcomes and execution health are recorded separately.

\subsection{Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1: Generated CSA checkers.} From identical starting artifacts, how do KNighter's actual loop and \toolname affect vulnerable-version warning coverage, fixed-side report burden and paired discrimination?

\textbf{RQ2: Cross-backend feasibility and cost.} Can a separately archived automatic CodeQL query be refined and independently replayed, and what integration surface and execution costs are observable?

\textbf{RQ3: Evidence sensitivity.} How do native-visible and no-internal pipelines differ on all 39 subjects and three paired decodes of the original 12-subject subset?

\textbf{RQ4: Model configuration sensitivity.} What outcomes arise on those 12 subjects under three editor configurations with common reply/output ceilings?

\subsection{Subjects}

E1 freezes one automatically generated CSA checker per selected Linux commit from KNighter's upstream artifacts. \subsection{Metrics}

Primary outcomes are vulnerable-version warning presence and fixed-side report burden. Let $n_v(D)$ and $n_f(D)$ count warnings on the same vulnerable/fixed patch-related object scope. The stronger secondary outcome PDS is:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
H_v(D,P,V)&=\mathbb{1}[\mathrm{alerts}(D,V_{\mathrm{vuln}},P)>0],\\
S_f(D,P,V)&=\mathbb{1}[\mathrm{alerts}(D,V_{\mathrm{fix}},P)=0],\\
\mathrm{PDS}(D,P,V)&=H_v(D,P,V)\land S_f(D,P,V).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:pds}
\end{equation}
PDS is not required for every useful edit. Recovery from silence may retain fixed noise; a warning-positive checker may improve by reducing fixed reports (Table~\ref{tab:terminology}).

For retained detector $D^*$ and normally paired-executed candidate $C$, the improvement rule is:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Accept}(C,D^*)={}&\mathrm{AnalyzerOK}(C)\land H_v(C)\\
&\land\bigl(\neg H_v(D^*)\lor n_f(C)<n_f(D^*)\bigr)\land Q(C).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:accept}
\end{equation}
The editor's local gate requires a changed artifact before paired assessment. $Q$ preserves required hits on controlled array fixtures in native; it is identically true for no-internal. This native-only guard records zero rejections in E1/E3, but its overhead and policy asymmetry remain disclosed. It is not a target-location oracle.

We binarize each checker/revision by warning presence. Each vulnerable revision is one positive and its fixed revision one negative; precision, recall and $F_1=2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)$ describe 78 patch-version decisions, not individual bug sites. Fixed reports measure inspection burden, not confirmed false-positive bugs. G08 warns on allocation failure rather than the registration-failure leak repaired by its patch; G24's remaining warnings have unverified target alignment. No exhaustive target-recall claim follows. Failed scans are unscored, never substituted as zero warnings.

\subsection{Experimental Environment}

CSA uses pinned Clang/LLVM 18.1.3 and Python 3.12.3; CodeQL uses 2.26.4, \texttt{codeql/cpp-all} 8.0.3 and no-build databases. Each Linux subject uses its patch commit and parent with \texttt{allyesconfig}, architecture-correct objects and identical starting/baseline/candidate scope. Manifests bind code, evidence and raw paired outputs. Requested model labels and high effort are retained; service labels do not establish immutable revisions. Configured temperature 0 is not enforced sampling control: both Responses adapters omit it. We claim no fixed seed or deterministic decoding.

\textbf{Generative AI in research.} LLMs generated starting checkers and edit candidates. Extraction, provenance checks, compilation, paired scans and numerical outcomes are programmatic. Codex assisted with drivers, audit/analysis code and perturbation preparation; it supplies no ground-truth target labels. Original messages, English reading companions, candidates and script hashes are retained; authors verify AI-assisted code and interpretations.

\subsection{Baselines and Variants}

E1 native, no-internal and KNighter share starting checkers, requested GPT-6-Luna high and a 32-reply ceiling. KNighter retains its five-report/two-outer-attempt/report-driven algorithm; its actual emitted artifacts receive independent scans. Its 24 no-report branches consume no reply. Two natural one-response No-FP stops are equivalence-audited reuses with their original eight-reply ceiling retained, not relabelled as fresh32-reply runs; termination occurs before either ceiling. The 15-case view uses these same outputs, not a favorable per-case allocation. Equal maximum replies do not mean equal actual calls, tokens or CPU work.

Within \toolname, no-internal withholds native compiler and checker-execution views while keeping checker, patch, frozen source context, build/paired feedback and stopping rule. Native receives bounded CFG witness/mismatch views and available dynamics; absence is explicit. E3 repeats the original 12 at 32 replies and 16,384 output tokens per call. E4 evaluates GPT-6-Luna high, GLM-5.3 high and GLM-5.3-Flash high with 2 replies and 65,536 output tokens per call. Actual GLM effort fields are retained. E4 is limited-budget configuration sensitivity, not equal-compute model ranking or cross-model native/control causality. Earlier default-effort, smaller-output and outside-subset histories stay separate.

\subsection{Protocol}

Each permitted reply produces a tool-mediated edit; compilable changed candidates receive independent paired replay. The next call starts from the latest attempted artifact and its failure/paired feedback, while the best retained detector is separate. Normal candidates follow Equation~\ref{eq:accept}. Stopping occurs at positive/fixed-silent saturation, three valid validations without improvement, three consecutive invalid model attempts, or the reply cap. Rejected edits, compile/format failures, observer unavailability, provider interruptions and invalid scans remain distinct.

Repairs preserve original failures and consumed prefixes. Thinking-only received replies are charged, not resent free. One GLM reply contained an explicit action and three edits but omitted explanatory summary; network-disabled replay supplied only an empty summary, reproduced exact model-specified code and made zero new calls. Healthy zero-callback probes use static evidence without invented traces. Repairs do not authorize manual scored-code edits, changed budgets or failed scans counted as negatives.
