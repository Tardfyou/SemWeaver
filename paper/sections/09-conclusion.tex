We presented \toolname, an automated post-synthesis checker editor with provenance-labelled compiler/checker-execution context and paired validation. On all 39 generated-only CSA subjects, its native GPT configuration reaches 23 warning-positive vulnerable revisions and version-level $F_1=0.575$, versus 15 and $0.448$ for KNighter's actual loop. Within the complete 15-case noisy stratum, it reduces fixed reports from KNighter's 69 to 44 while retaining all vulnerable warning signals, primarily through one subject. Overall native noise increases and $F_1$ nearly ties no-internal; repeated comparisons show conditional, sampling-dependent benefits. A separate automatic CodeQL example and three-model subset provide bounded feasibility and sensitivity evidence. The contribution is an auditable checker-completion workflow and its observed tradeoffs, not universal precision improvement, target-recall proof or unseen-site generalization.
