We presented \toolname, a post-synthesis refinement layer that combines analyzer evidence, patch context and paired validation to refine LLM-generated vulnerability checkers.
On 39 KNighter-derived Linux checkers, it recovers more vulnerable-version warning signals than KNighter's report-driven loop, while nearly matching no-internal $F_1$ and producing more fixed-side reports overall.
The complete non-tie audit and selected follow-up experiments explain both useful local repairs and the conditions they leave unresolved.
Repeated model decodes and controlled source changes also show where those repairs vary or fail to transfer across source forms.
Analyzer evidence can help a checker recognize the relevant operation; deciding whether that operation is unsafe still requires the right object, guard, path and scope.
