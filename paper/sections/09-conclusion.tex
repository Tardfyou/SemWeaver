\toolname contributes an auditable post-synthesis checker editor with provenance-labelled compiler/checker context and paired validation. Across 39 generated-only CSA subjects, native GPT reaches 23 warning-positive vulnerable revisions and version-level $F_1=0.575$, versus KNighter's 15 and $0.448$. In the complete 15-case noisy stratum, fixed reports fall from KNighter's 69 to 44 with all vulnerable warning signals retained, primarily through one subject. Overall native noise increases, $F_1$ nearly ties no-internal, and repeated benefits depend on sampling. A separate automatic CodeQL example and three-model subset provide bounded feasibility and sensitivity evidence. These are conditional checker-completion tradeoffs, not universal precision gains, target-recall proof or unseen-site generalization.
